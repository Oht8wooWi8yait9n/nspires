Special Notices & Amendments

Notices Amendment 9 to the NASA ARMD Research Opportunities in Aeronautics (ROA) 2010 NRA has been posted on the NSPIRES site. This release restarts the solicitation for Hypersonics research that was originally issued as Amendment 4, HYP1. Due to funding reductions for Hypersonics research in FY2012 and beyond, not all of the topics solicited under Amendment 4 will be funded. Step-2 proposals for HYP1-2 are due August 22, 2011. The due date for Step-2 proposals to solicitation NNH10ZEA001N-HYP1 &lt;http://nspires.nasaprs.com/external/solicitations/summary.do?method=init&amp;solId=%7bF47C835F-5DE7-53B6-97C8-6282DB2A867F%7d&amp;path=open&gt; has been suspended until further notice. The FY2012 President's Budget request reduces funding for hypersonics research by 50% compared to the FY2011 level. The Hypersonics Project is therefore replanning the future investment portfolio in hypersonics research. The Project anticipates lifting the suspension for some subtopics in the solicitation but canceling other subtopics all together. We will notify you of the subtopics for which funding will be available and of the new proposal due date via a future e-mail. Questions should be directed to the POC for this solicitation, Mr. Karl Vaden, at Hypersonics@lists.nasa.gov (Posted 3/3/2011) The decision date for downselect of STEP-1 proposals submitted to the Hypersonics Project solicitation (Appendix A.5) within the NASA ARMD Research Opportunities in Aeronautics (ROA2010) has been changed to January 7, 2011. All who submitted Step-1 proposals will be informed by NASA as to whether or not they are invited to submit a full Step-2 proposal on January 7, 2011. Amendment 4 to the NASA ARMD Research Opportunities in Aeronautics (ROA) 2010 NRA has been posted on the NSPIRES site. This release includes sixteen topics in Appendix A.5 for foundational research on reusable airbreathing access to space topics and entry/descent/landing topics in support of the Hypersonics Project. Mandatory Step-1 proposals (white papers) are due November 23, 2010.
