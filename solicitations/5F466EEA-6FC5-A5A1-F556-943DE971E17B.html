Special Notices & Amendments

Notices NOTICE: Amended July 24, 2026. This amendment announces the Step-2 due date for this program element, which was previously TBD, is now September 25, 2026. Step-2 proposals can only be submitted by those who submitted a Step-1 proposal and were invited to submit a Step-2. Also, the requirements for Biographical Sketches and Current and Pending Support have been updated, see the table of Requirements for Expertise and Resources Not Anonymized (E&amp;R) Document on page 11 in Section 3.2. New text is in bold. NOTICE: Research security training required starting August 5, 2026. All PIs and any Co-Is that would spend &ge; 10% time on a proposed grants or cooperative agreements must certify they have taken research security training. NASA will be satisfied with the four online research security training modules on the National Science Foundation's (NSF) Research Security Training website at https://www.nsf.gov/research-security/training or the SECURE Center condensed version of the four modules at https://www.secure-center.org/ctm NOTICE: April 15, 2026. The FAQ for this program has been updated. See Heliophysics Foundational Research Frequently Asked Questions (.pdf) under other documents on the right side of this NSPIRES page. NOTICE: March 16, 2026. Frequently Asked Questions for Heliophysics Foundational Research has been posted under ' Other Documents' to the right on this NSPIRES page. NOTICE: A preproposal telecon for B.2 Heliophysics Foundational Research will occur February 27, 2026, 1:00-2:00 PM Eastern Time. Submit questions by February 25, 2026, at 11:59 pm ET, via https://forms.gle/ju9jMazbLaJBdwWRA and the connect information for the February 27 telecon will be posted under other documents on the right side of this NSPIRES page. NOTICE: Amended February 11, 2026. This amendment makes two corrections to this program element: The length of the Step-1 Proposal in Section 3.3.1 has been corrected from two pages to three, and the expected budget numbers given in the summary table are per award, per year, not the budget for all of the new awards in the SST. An additional POC was also added. New text is in bold and deleted text is struck through. The Step-1 due date remains May 1, 2026. Proposals submitted to this program will be evaluated using a dual-anonymous review process. Proposals must be prepared according to the guidelines in Section 2.4.2 and in the associated &quot;Guidelines for Proposers to ROSES DAPR Programs&quot; document under &quot;Other Documents&quot; on the NSPIRES page for this program element. All proposers are strongly encouraged to use the standard SMD template for the Open Science Data Management Plan see https://science.nasa.gov/researchers/sara/faqs/OSDMP The description of the specific proposal opportunity on this page is contained in the document 'B.2 Heliophysics Foundational Research'.
