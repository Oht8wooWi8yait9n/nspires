This program element has requirements that differ from and supersede the defaults in the ROSES Summary of Solicitation and the Proposer&rsquo;s Guide, e.g., cost sharing (Sections 3.1.5 and 3.1.6), the 3-page &quot;End User Description Form&quot; (Section 4.8), and use of the Earth Science Division&rsquo;s template for work effort and current and pending support, see Section 4.6. The completed End User Description Form must be uploaded as a separate document within the NSPIRES submission system. Proposers intending to use Grants.gov in lieu of NSPIRES must contact Keith Gaddis Keith.Gaddis@NASA.gov and cc sara@nasa.gov at least 30 days in advance of the proposal due date.
