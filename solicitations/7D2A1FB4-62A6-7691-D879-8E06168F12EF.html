Special Notices & Amendments

Notices NOTICE: Research security training required starting August 5, 2026. All PIs and any Co-Is that would spend &ge; 10% time on a proposed grants or cooperative agreements must certify they have taken research security training. NASA will be satisfied with the four online research security training modules on the National Science Foundation's (NSF) Research Security Training website at https://www.nsf.gov/research-security/training or the SECURE Center condensed version of the four modules at https://www.secure-center.org/ctm NOTICE: Amended February 5, 2026. The Step-2 proposal due date has been delayed to February 27, 2026, to allow time for proposers to make proposal and budgetary modifications due to the adjustment to the period of performance for south polar investigations. NOTICE: Amended January 30, 2026: This amendment updates text about the Architecture Definition Document in Section 1, including new Data Gaps and updated Technology Gaps that proposals may address. Tables F.10-1 and F.10-2 were updated to include the Safety and Mission Assurance plan and the Master Equipment List and/or Power Equipment List appendices. Section 5 contains new guidance for Period of Performance budgeting for south polar investigations. New text is in bold and deleted text is struck through. NOTICE: Amended December 15, 2025: This amendment adds two additional items under subsection 2.4.2.4 &quot;Additional Required Content&quot;: -- a required Safety and Mission Assurance plan, and an optional extended Master Equipment List and/or Power Equipment List. New text is in bold. NOTICE: F.10 PRISM FAQ updated on 12112025 NOTICE: Corrected December 5, 2025. A bullet was added to the list of information in Section 4.1 that proposers must provide if leveraging survive-the-night services. NOTICE: Corrected November 24, 2025. Initially the date given for the pre-proposal conference Section 9 was December 10. That was an error. The date for the pre-proposal conference is December 5, 2025. NOTICE: Amended November 24, 2025. This amendment defers three dates for this program element: The pre-proposal webinar will occur December 5, 2025, at 1 pm Eastern Time, see the PPC connect information in Section 9, Step-1 proposals are now due December 12, 2025, and Step-2 proposals are now due February 20, 2026. Moreover, a few other changes were made: In Section 2.1 a paragraph about the direct purchase of goods, supplies and/or services from non-U.S. sources was expanded, small changes were made to Section 3.1 to provide updated guidance on landing site hazard analyses, and a paragraph referring to cooperative agreements as the award type was removed from Section 5. New text is in bold, deleted text is struck through. FAQs will be posted shortly under &quot;Other documents&quot; on the right side of this NSPIRES page. NOTICE: Amended October 29, 2025. This amendment changes to &quot;TBD&quot; the Step-1 proposal due date for this program. The solicitation &quot;close date&quot; shown in NSPIRES is NOT a new proposal due date; that is merely an artefact of the NSPIRES system. When funding is reinstated, an amendment will be issued setting a new Step-1 proposal due date. We anticipate that proposers to programs that were set to TBD will be given back as many business days as the government was shut down NOTICE: October 13, 2025. The pre-proposal conference for F.10 PRISM previously scheduled for October 15, 2025, at 2 pm Eastern Time has been changed to TBD as a result of the continuing government shutdown. NOTICE: Amended September 30, 2025. This amendment releases final text for this program element, which was previously TBD. There will be a Pre-proposal Conference on October 15, 2025, at 2 pm Eastern Time, see the PPC connect information in Section 9. Step-1 proposals are due November 5, 2025, and Step-2 proposals are due January 30, 2026. NOTICE: Amended July 21, 2025. The short description of this TBD opportunity has been expanded and a draft schedule provided. New text is in bold.
