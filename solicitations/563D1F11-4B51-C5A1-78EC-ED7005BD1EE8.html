Special Notices & Amendments

Notices No selections were made for this program. The description of the specific proposal opportunity on this page is contained in the document 'D.11 FUSE Legacy Science Program as amended.' This document is kept up to date and incorporates amendments in a clearly identifiable manner. ROSES-2007 is an omnibus NASA Research Announcement. It contains over 50 different proposal opportunities. The document 'Summary of Solicitation' describes the common requirements for all ROSES-2007 proposal opportunities; all proposers must satisfy the proposal requirements in the 'Summary of Solicitation'. The documents 'Table 2' and 'Table 3' contain the list of all proposal opportunities and their due dates. The document 'Research Program Overview' describes research activities within the NASA science division that is managing the specific proposal opportunity on this page. The document 'D.11 FUSE Legacy Science Program as amended' describes the specific proposal opportunity on this page. All of these documents are kept up to date and incorporate amendments in a clearly identifiable manner. D.11: Amendment 13 on July 18, 2007.
