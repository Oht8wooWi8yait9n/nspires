Notices NOTICE: Amended October 1, 2026. This amendment releases a second opportunity to propose to the NuSTAR observatory in ROSES-25. NuSTAR General Observer (GO) Cycle 12 was previously solicited in Section 4. This amendment invites proposals for NuSTAR General Observer Cycle 13 as in Section 11. The due date for Phase-1 Proposals (submitted via ARK RPS) for is January 28, 2027. NOTICE: Corrected September 8, 2026: The Swift joint observations with IXPE have been removed, see Section 9. Deleted text is struck through. The due date for IXPE Phase-1 Proposals (submitted via ARK RPS) remains September 17, 2026. NOTICE: Amended July 27, 2026. The work plan previously included in the anonymized scientific justification Section of Phase-1 proposals must now be provided in the Expertise and Resources Not Anonymized document. New text is in bold, see Sections 9.5 and 10.4. The Phase-1 Proposal due date (submitted via ARK RPS) for both IXPE and NICER remains unchanged at September 17, 2026. NOTICE: Research security training required starting August 5, 2026. All PIs and any Co-Is that would spend &ge; 10% time on a proposed grants or cooperative agreements must certify they have taken research security training. NASA will be satisfied with the four online research security training modules on the National Science Foundation's (NSF) Research Security Training website at https://www.nsf.gov/research-security/training or the SECURE Center condensed version of the four modules at https://www.secure-center.org/ctm NOTICE: May 21, 2026. A newly established joint program with XMM-Newton was added to the IXPE General Observer Cycle 4 proposals, see Section 9. New text is in bold. The due date for Phase-1 Proposals (submitted via ARK RPS) remains September 17, 2026. NOTICE: Amended May 15, 2026. This amendment releases a second opportunity to propose to the IXPE and NICER observatories in ROSES-25. NICER General Observer (GO) Cycle 8 was previously solicited in Section 6 and IXPE GO -- Cycle 3 was previously solicited in Section 7. This amendment invites proposals for IXPE General Observer Cycle 4 (see Section 9) and NICER General Observer Cycle 9 (see Section 10). The due date for Phase-1 Proposals (submitted via ARK RPS) for both IXPE and NICER is September 17, 2026. NOTICE: Amended December 22, 2025. This amendment releases final text for TESS General Investigator (GI) and announces that the Phase-1 proposals are due March 10, 2026 (via ARK RPS). See Section 5, new text is in bold. NOTICE: Amended, December 12, 2025. Instructions in Section 9.1 about how to request high-end computing have been modified. New text is in bold and deleted text is struck through. NOTICE: Amended, December 10, 2025. This amendment releases final text and due dates for XRISM General Observer, which was previously TBD. Both the Type-1 Phase-1 due date and the Type-2 (NSPIRES) proposal due date for this program are February 27, 2026. Revised text includes opportunities for new joint observations with XMM-Newton and NuSTAR, allowing coordinated or simultaneous studies across observatories when scientifically justified. Additionally, time-constrained and Target-of-Opportunity (TOO) observations, up to a combined maximum of 20% of total observing time are also allowed. See Section 8. NOTICE: Amended November 19, 2025. In Section 4, NuSTAR, three changes have been made: joint observations with XRISM have been added, joint observations with NICER have been removed, and the Technical POC for NuSTAR has been updated. New text is in bold and deleted text is struck through. The due date for Phase-1 proposals (via ARK RPS) remains January 29, 2026. NOTICE: Amended August 25, 2025. The Programmatic POC for Swift has been updated, see Section 2.2. New text is in bold. NOTICE: Amended July 30, 2025. On June 17, 2025, NICER science operations were suspended due to a hardware anomaly in the pointing system. A rich data analysis opportunity remains, see Section 6.
