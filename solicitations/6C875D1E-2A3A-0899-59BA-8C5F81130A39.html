Special Notices & Amendments

Notices NOTICE: September 22, 2023 the Vigil FMO AO has been amended. Updated text is provided in the revised amendment document posted on this page and a summary of the changes is provided in the &quot;Amendment 1&quot; document also posted on this page. Changes are clearly marked in the AO document and summarized near the beginning. The proposal due date is unchanged. Slides from the optional, July 13th virtual Preproposal Conference are available on the Vigil FMO Acquisition site (see link below). Individual presentations can be found by clicking on the links on the Agenda next to each presenter&rsquo;s name. Link: See Details The optional, virtual Preproposal Conference (PPC) is scheduled for July 13, 2023. The agenda and access information is available on the VFMO Acquisition Page at the link to the right (under other documents) and on this NSPIRES page under Other Documents. The Pre-Proposal Conference has been scheduled from 10:00 AM to 2:30 PM Eastern Daylight Time (EDT/UTC-04:00) on Thursday, July 13, 2023. Participation will be via Webex (presentation materials, audio, and text and verbal questions) and teleconference (audio and verbal questions). No travel is required to attend the conference as it will be completely virtual. Potential proposers to the Vigil FMO Announcement of Opportunity (AO) are encouraged to participate in this Pre-Proposal Conference. Information will be presented by NASA and ESA officials. Participants will have the opportunity to pose questions regarding the AO.
