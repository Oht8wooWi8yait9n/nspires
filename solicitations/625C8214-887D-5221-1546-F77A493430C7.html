Special Notices & Amendments

Notices Amendment 10, released September 26, 2018, makes seven changes in Sections, 4, 6 and 8, including modifying previously published restrictions on changing team membership after the notification proposal has been submitted. Amendment 8 released August 28, 2018 changes Notification Proposal and Proposal due dates and the CD-ROM delivery date. NOTICE: Amended August 20, 2018. This amendment makes two changes to this PEA: Requirement M-25 in Section 5.6.1 refers specifically to New Missions using Existing Spacecraft (NMES) and in Section 9 it is noted that the date for Preproposal Conference has been changed to August 24. New text is in bold and deleted text is struck through. A Pre-proposal Web Conference for HPSMO is scheduled for August 24, 2018. Schedule updates, agenda, and logistical information can be found on the Acquisition homepage for the 2018 Heliophysics Science MO at https://soma.larc.nasa.gov/2018HelioMO/ August 7, 2018: Amendment 6 to SALMON 3 released this new proposal opportunity via PEA M 2018 Heliophysics Science Mission of Opportunity . The Notice of Intent is replaced by submission of a Notification Proposal. Full proposals will not be accepted if a notification proposal was not submitted. The due dates are provided in the Table of Due dates, above, and in the PEA document.
