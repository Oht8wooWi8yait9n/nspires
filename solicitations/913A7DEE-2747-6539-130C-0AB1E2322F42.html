Special Notices & Amendments

Notices ****IMPORTANT NOTICE FOR SELECTED FIs******. If you have read in the relevant selection document that you are a selected Future Investigator, but have not received personalized selection information, you still must confirm acceptance (or not) by JULY 9. Please see the introductory text in the funding division&rsquo;s selection document for instructions on how to email your confirmation. Amendment 3 Jun 24, 2019: Changes to progress report submission, point of contact, and expected date for formal notification of non-selection NOTE: NSPIRES generates an automatic acknowledgement when any proposal is submitted on time or late. When the FINESST solicitation is totally shut down on NSPIRES, the proposer is prevented from finishing a submission. If the university did not receive notice confirming submission of a proposal, check spam filters and junk boxes. If unable to locate the e-mail acknowledgement, then contact the NSPIRES Help Desk or log in directly to NSPIRES to check a submission status. See also Section 4.2 Proposal Submission Due Dates: SMD does not pre-approve the submission of a late proposal. The decision to submit a late proposal is solely that of the proposer, and it is then NASA&rsquo;s decision whether to accept it or not. The FINESST program officers/administrators are not empowered to authorize the submission of a late proposal. NOTICE: Amendment 2 on February 7, 2019 changes the proposal due date to March 11, 2019. It also changes, clarifies, and updates the solicitation in several ways. Proposers are advised to look at the solicitation document and FAQ documents carefully for all updates. NOTICE: Amended on January 25, 2019. This amendment changes the proposal due date for Future Investigators in NASA Earth and Space Science and Technology (FINESST) from February 1, 2019 to TBD. A new date will be set when the government reopens, with some additional time provided since some proposers have been unable to work. Please note that the due date shown in NSPIRES is NOT a proposal due date; the system requires a specific date be used rather than 'TBD'. Notice: November 20, 2018. Minor corrections were made to the solicitation document (see updated document posted above) and the FAQ was updated. Please check the FAQ periodically, as it will continue to be updated as new questions are answered. Please note that this FINESST program is a new program that replaces the NESSF solicitation for new proposers. However, current NESSF fellows needing to submit renewal proposals should do so via the NESSF19R solicitation. Link: See Details
