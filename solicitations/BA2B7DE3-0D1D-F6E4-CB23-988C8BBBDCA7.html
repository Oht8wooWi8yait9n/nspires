Special Notices & Amendments

Notices For the Subsonic Fixed Wing Project, the due date for proposals to be considered in the first evaluation period is 5/11/07. To be considered in this round of evaluations, proposals must be received by 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time as described in Section IV(c). It is expected that the majority, and perhaps all, of the available funds will be allocated during this round of evaluations. However, the topics in this section will remain open until a final due date of 7/6/07 in case funding still remains after this round of evaluations. A note will be posted on the ROA page on NSPIRES if funds are expended and no further reviews are expected. Proposers who decide to wait until after the first round of evaluations do so at their own risk. Proposers are encouraged to read Appendix A.2. The Subsonic Fixed Wing (SFW) Project has received many high-quality proposals for the topics released in Amendment 4 of the Research Opportunities in Aeronautics. As a result, the Project expects to expend its budgeted funding for these topics. Consistent with the note under Table 2 and 3 and Appendix A.2 of the NRA, this posting announces that the Project does not intend to review any further proposals submitted under Amendment 4. July 3, 2007.
