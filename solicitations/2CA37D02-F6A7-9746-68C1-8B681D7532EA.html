Special Notices & Amendments

Notices NOTICE: Slides from the FINESST Earth Office Hours December 20, 2023, and January 10, 2024 have been posted under &ldquo;Other documents&rdquo; on the lower right of this NSPIRES page. NOTICE: January 4, 2023. Several corrections and clarifications have been made to FINESST-2023: &bull; Section 2.5 &quot;Biological and Physical Sciences Research Program&quot; (BPS) has been updated to cite Biophysics and states that proposals relevant to BPS program elements that are not explicitly mentioned in Section 2.5 are eligible. &bull; Section &quot;5.1 Review&quot; has been updated to include a direct reference to the assessment of the Open Science Data Management Plan (OSDMP) or the explanation for why no OSDMP is necessary. &bull; A new section number &quot;12.39&quot; explains period of performance options, pre-award costing, and the expected timing for award funding to reach the institution. &bull; Section 13.15 adds a link to instructions for how to upload manuscripts to the NASA-designated publication repository and confirms that as accepted manuscript versions must be uploaded by the time of publication. The due date for FINESST-23 remains unchanged: proposals are due February 6, 2024. NOTICE: Amended November 6, 2023. This Amendment releases the final text for this program that solicits graduate student research, which had been listed as &quot;TBD&quot;. Notices of Intent may not be submitted. Proposals are due February 6, 2024. An optional, pre-proposal teleconference will occur on December 1, 2023, at 3:30 PM Eastern Time, see Section 12.8. Although structured as Question and Answers (Q&amp;A), Sections 12 &amp; 13 will be legally binding on awardees. The short URL for the NSPIRES page for this program element is https://go.nasa.gov/FINESST23 The description of the specific proposal opportunity on this page is contained in the document 'F.5 Future Investigators in NASA Earth and Space Science and Technology'. The document 'F.1 Cross Division Research Overview' describes research activities within the NASA science division that is managing the specific proposal opportunity on this page and may impose requirements upon proposals submitted to this program element. The document 'Summary of Solicitation' describes the common requirements for all ROSES-2023 proposal opportunities. The document 'Table 1' contains the proposal check list from the Summary of Solicitation. The documents 'Table 2' and 'Table 3' contain the list of all proposal opportunities and their due dates, sorted by (full or Step-2) proposal due date or appendix number, respectively. All of these documents are kept up to date and incorporate amendments, clarifications, and corrections in a clearly identifiable manner.
