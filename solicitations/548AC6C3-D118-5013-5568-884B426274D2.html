Special Notices & Amendments

Notices NOTICE: Amended on September 19, 2014. To allow proposers adequate time to prepare their full Step-2 proposals after hearing the decision about their Step-1 proposals, the due date for Step-2 proposals for this program is now October 27, 2014. NOTICE: Amended on August 11, 2014. This amendment delays the due date for Step-1 proposals by one week to August 21, 2014. The due date for Step-2 proposals is unchanged, it remains October 9, 2014. The text of C.20, the Dawn at Ceres Guest Investigator Program, was clarified on August 6, 2014 to provide explicit instructions for foreign investigators in Section 2.1 and to relax part of the requirement in Section 2.2 that all data be in the public domain. The proposal due dates remain unchanged. Amended on July 9, 2014. This amendment presents final text and a new name for C.20, now called the Dawn at Ceres Guest Investigator Program. Proposals to this program will be taken by a two-step process, in which the Notice of Intent is replaced by a required Step-1 proposal submitted by an Authorized Organizational Representative. Only proposers who submit a Step-1 proposal are eligible to submit a Step-2 (full) proposal. See Section 2.3 for details. Step-1 proposals are due August 14, 2014, and Step-2 proposals are due October 9, 2014. The &quot;Rules of the Road&quot; and &quot;Proposal Information Package (PIP)&quot; documents referenced in the solicitation will not be finalized before the week of July 14. They will be posted above under &quot;Other Documents&quot; as soon as possible. Amended on May 20, 2014. This amendment announces that C.20, The Dawn Focused Research and Analysis Program (DFRAP), which has been listed as TBD since ROSES-14 was released in February 2014, will not be offered in this form. Instead, the scope of C.11, the Discovery Data Analysis Program (DDAP) has been expanded to include the Dawn mission data. C.3, Solar System Workings (SSW), was always open to eligible studies of Dawn. SSW due dates remain unchanged, Step-1 proposals are due May 23, 2014, and Step-2 proposals are due July 25, 2014. DDAP due dates remain unchanged. Step-1 proposals are due July 21, 2014, and Step-2 proposals are due September 19, 2014. NASA intends to issue a Dawn Guest Investigator Program through C.20 by an Amendment to ROSES-2014. Instructions for how to submit Step-1 proposals have been included under &quot;other documents&quot;. Please note, Step-1 proposals are treated as proposals in the system and, as such, you must following the instructions for submitting a proposal, not a Notice of Intent. For example, AORs must approve your Step-1 proposal submission. The description of the specific proposal opportunity on this page is contained in the document 'C.20 Dawn Focused Research and Analysis'. This document is kept up to date and incorporates amendments, clarifications and corrections in a clearly identifiable manner. ROSES-2014 is an omnibus NASA Research Announcement. It contains over 50 different proposal opportunities. The documents 'Table 2' and 'Table 3' contain the list of all proposal opportunities and their due dates.
