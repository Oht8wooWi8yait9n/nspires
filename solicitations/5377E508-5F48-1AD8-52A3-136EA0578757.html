Special Notices & Amendments

Notices Intending proposers to the 2019 Astrophysics Small Explorer (SMEX) Announcement of Opportunity (AO) or to the 2019 Astrophysics Explorers Missions of Opportunity Program Element Appendix for the Third Stand Alone Missions of Opportunity Notice (SALMON-3) AO, both released in draft form on November 6, 2018, should be aware that a major update to the QAs documents for both announcements was released on 11 December 2018. Those documents are available by following the links on the Astrophysics Explorers Program Acquisition websites at https://explorers.larc.nasa.gov/2019APSMEX/SMEX and https://explorers.larc.nasa.gov/2019APSMEX/MO​. The comment period for the Draft 2019 SMEX AO and the Draft 2019 APEXMO PEA has been extended to December 21, 2018. The comment period for the Draft 2019 Astrophysics SMEX AO ends on December 7, 2018. Comments on the draft may be addressed via email to the Astrophysics Explorers Program Scientist: Dr. Linda S. Sparke (subject line to read &quot;2019 SMEX AO&quot;) at: linda.s.sparke@nasa.gov
