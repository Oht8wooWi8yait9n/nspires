Special Notices & Amendments

Notices NOTICE: Amended on December 8, 2014. This amendment announces that C.19, which has been listed as TBD since ROSES-2014 was released in February 2014, will not be solicited in 2014. However, it will be released as part of ROSES-2015. July 1, 2014. The Planetary Science Division plans to release the text of this call in December 2014 and the due date for Step-2 proposals will be no fewer than 90 days after the release of final text. NASA anticipates that instrument scientist awards would receive funding starting in FY 2016 and the Sample Scientist awards would start with a small amount in 2017. Instructions for how to submit Step-1 proposals have been included under &quot;other documents&quot;. Please note, Step-1 proposals are treated as proposals in the system and, as such, you must following the instructions for submitting a proposal, not a Notice of Intent. For example, AORs must approve your Step-1 proposal submission. The description of the specific proposal opportunity on this page is contained in the document 'C.19 Hayabusa2 Participating Scientist'.
