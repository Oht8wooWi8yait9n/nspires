Special Notices & Amendments

Notices NOTICE: The NASA Science Mission Directorate Science Activation program (originally solicited as Science Education Cooperative Agreement Notice NNH15ZDA004C) is NOT soliciting new proposals. Only those Principal Investigators who were previously selected and have been asked to submit an extension budget should submit a cover page and documentation in accordance with the instructions provided to them. IMPORTANT: For those offerors submitting proposals, all questions in the &quot;Program Specific Data&quot; section are required, except Questions 1, 6, and 7.
