Special Notices & Amendments

Notices This notice is being made available to all potential sources and may be of particular interest to those in attendance at the NASA Environmentally Responsible Aviation (ERA) Project N+2 Advanced Vehicle Concepts NRA Pre-Proposal Meeting held on February 19th, 2010 in Washington, DC. In the intervening period since the Pre-Proposal Meeting, NASA has decided to make some fairly substantial changes to the solicitation and wants to provide this update:  The Presidents FY11 Budget Request included a proposed budget augmentation for ERA vehicle design studies. Accordingly, a ROA2010 solicitation will be issued that will focus on developing vehicle concepts that have high potential to meet all three NASA N+2 subsonic transport system level metrics simultaneously to Preliminary Design, which is further into the design cycle than previously planned. Previously, as explained at the February 19, 2010 ERA NRA Pre-Proposal Meeting, the solicitation was planned to be focused only on conceptual designs.  Ground testing of the vehicle concepts (risk reduction) where approaches such as hybrid laminar flow control (HLFC) systems and propulsion-airframe-integration tests are needed will be identified and performed as an integral part of the study, along with traditional low speed and transonic test cycles usually performed as part of preliminary design. Separate NRA Quick Start Topics, as discussed at the Pre-Proposal Meeting, will no longer be part of this ERA Solicitation.  Cost sharing will be expected for a significant portion of the study, which will be about 24 months in duration. At this time, it is anticipated that government funds available will be approximately $36M in total. The anticipated release date for the ROA2010 solicitation is late April/early May. Please direct questions to Fay Collier at Fayette.S.Collier@nasa.gov &lt;Fayette.S.Collier@nasa.gov&gt;. The National Aeronautics and Space Administration (NASA), Aeronautics Research Mission Directorate's (ARMD) Environmentally Responsible Aviation (ERA) Project under the Integrated Systems Research Program (ISRP) is hosting a NASA Research Announcement (NRA) Pre-Proposal Meeting on Thursday February 11, 2010 in Washington, D.C. to share information on the advanced vehicle concepts and technologies of interest and to seek input from the broad aeronautics community to refine the upcoming N+2 Advanced Vehicle Concepts NRA solicitation. A draft of the solicitation is expected to be released one week prior to the NRA Pre-Proposal Meeting. Further information on this meeting may be found at http://www.aeronautics.nasa.gov/calendar/20100211.htm &lt;http://www.aeronautics.nasa.gov/calendar/20100211.htm&gt; Amendment 8 to the NASA ARMD Research Opportunities in Aeronautics (ROA) 2009 NRA has been posted on the NSPIRES site. Amendment 7 to the NASA ARMD Research Opportunities in Aeronautics (ROA) 2009 NRA has been posted on the NSPIRES site. This release includes topics for the Aviation Safety Program and is funded by the American Recovery and Reinvestment Act (ARRA) of 2009. This release includes three topics for the Aging Aircraft and Durability Project and four topics for the new Verification and Validation of Flight Critical Systems Project within the Aviation Safety Program. NASA intends to utilize the Research Opportunities in Aeronautics (ROA) NRA to also solicit for some proposals that will be funded through the American Recovery and Reinvestment Act (ARRA). For several years NASA Aeronautics has worked to localize research funding opportunities in one solicitation (ROA) that is well understood by the community. Perspective proposers should carefully review each topic in the Appendices to see if the ARRA guidelines apply. NASA intends to use a sequential numbering for amendments, but for clarity all topics in an amendment will utilize ARRA funds or normally appropriated funds. Due to the need to include special information for synopses that provide information on ARRA activities, NASA will use solicitation numbers for the ROA 2009-ROA. NNH09ZEA001N is used to reference the overall NRA while, NNH09ZEA001N-1 is also used to indicate information related to ARRA activities. Both numbers are linked to this single solicitation. NASA intends that funds provided through the American Recovery and Reinvestment Act (ARRA) of 2009 will be utilized to fund selected proposals. In order to provide additional transparency of the use of these funds, all recipients of ARRA funds are required to adhere to additional reporting requirements. The Terms and Conditions provided in the ROA NRA (#NNH09ZEA001N) are applicable to this Recovery Act Funded Appendix. For Recovery Act Funded Appendices, the Terms and Conditions applicable to NNH09ZEA001N are augmented by the Recovery Act specific Provisions. Proposers are encouraged to become familiar with the reporting requirements and ensure that the appropriate processes and budget (e.g., allowance for any additional administrative costs) to execute these requirements are included in any proposal that may be funded through the ARRA. This release includes the following three topics for the Subsonic Rotary Wing Project: 1) development of tools for computational modeling of high speed gearbox windage losses; 2) design, fabrication, and evaluation of active boundary layer flow control for on-blade alleviation of retreating blade stall during moderate forward speeds in a relevant, rotating blade environment; 3) demonstration of concepts for reducing high frequency cabin noise in helicopters due to airborne and structure-borne excitation of the airframe through application of advanced structural design or active control.
