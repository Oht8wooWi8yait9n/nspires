Notices Note: This NOFO is open only to U.S. 4-year colleges/universities, designated by the U.S. Department of Education as Minority Serving Institutions (AANH, AANAPISI, HBCU, HSI, NASNTI, PBI, TCU). Institutions not meeting this requirement are encouraged to collaborate with a lead MSI. Successful MIRO proposals will be funded as multi-year cooperative agreements with a five-year period of performance. Notice of Intent (optional) due at 11:59 pm on January 12, 2024. Full Proposals due at 11:59 pm Eastern Time on February 14, 2024.
