Special Notices & Amendments

Notices Please note that a synopsis has been posted for the upcoming release of ROA 2009. It is expected that the ROA 2009 solicitation will be available on or about April 10, 2009. Amendment 8 to the NASA ARMD Research Opportunities in Aeronautics (ROA) 2008 NRA has been posted on the NSPIRES site. The amendment includes a new research topic for Appendix B-2 (The Aircraft Aging and Durability Project). This topic is related to exploring and documenting a methodology to establish rigorous risk-based models for assessing damage detection capability in maintenance and repair operations. Amendment 7 to the NASA ARMD Research Opportunities in Aeronautics (ROA) 2008 NRA has been posted on the NSPIRES site. The amendment includes two new subtopics for the Supersonics Project related to systems level validation testing, which leverages the substantial investments made by the project (both internally and through the NRA). NSPIRES was updated to reflect the due dates posted in Amendment 6 to the NASA ARMD Research Opportunities in Aeronautics (ROA) 2008 NRA. The dates listed on NSPIRES now match Table 2 and 3 of the NRA. The topics listed in Appendix C-2 and C-3 have an NOI due date of 6/23/08 and a proposal due date of 7/23/08. Amendment 6 to the NASA ARMD Research Opportunities in Aeronautics (ROA) 2008 NRA has been posted on the NSPIRES site. The amendment includes new topics for the NextGen-Airspace and NextGen-Airportal Projects. Amendment 5 to the NASA ARMD Research Opportunities in Aeronautics (ROA) 2008 NRA has been posted on the NSPIRES site. The amendment includes a new research topic that is cross-program in scope. The purpose of the Innovation in Aeronautics Instruction Topic is to encourage universities or non-profit organizations to create and implement cutting-edge innovative instructional programs in aeronautical engineering or related fields. Amendment 4 to the NASA ARMD Research Opportunities in Aeronautics (ROA) 2008 NRA has been posted on the NSPIRES site. This purpose of this amendment is to correct one piece of information in the Aircraft Aging and Durability Project Appendix (B-2). No new topics are added in this amendment. Amendment 3 to the NASA ARMD Research Opportunities in Aeronautics (ROA) 2008 NRA has been posted on the NSPIRES site. The amendment includes a new research topic for the NextGen-Airportal Project. The JPDO NextGen concept and the NASA NextGen Airportal Project envision future airport systems that include more demanding performance requirements, increased levels of optimization and automation, increased information requirements and system complexity, integrated displays, decision-aiding software and revised allocation of tasks between the human and automation and between the Air Navigation Service Provider (ANSP) and the aircraft operators. The main objective of the requested research is to provide a specific, repeatable method for assessing the assignment of roles and functions to humans and automated systems, and apply it in designated operational concepts Amendment 2 to the NASA ARMD Research Opportunities in Aeronautics (ROA) 2008 NRA has been posted on the NSPIRES site. The amendment includes new research topics for the Aircraft Aging and Durability Project (AAD), the Integrated Vehicle Health Management Project (IVHM), the Subsonic Fixed Wing Project (SFW), and the Supersonics Project (SUP). The topics posted for the SFW and SUP projects are for the Advanced Concept Studies For Subsonic Commercial Transport Aircraft Entering Service in the 2030-2035 Period topic that was covered in a pre-proposal conference on November 29, 2007. With these topics, NASA is seeking to stimulate innovation and foster the pursuit of revolutionary conceptual designs for aircraft that could enter into service in the 2030-35 period, market permitting. In support of NextGen research, the IVHM topics include extensions to health monitoring of airspace systems and of software systems, in addition to those more focused on IVHM on-board technology for detection, diagnosis, and prognosis of in-flight events. Research topics for the AAD project include the following foundational and discipline areas: Sensing &amp; Diagnostic Technologies, Continuum-based Models and Computational Methods, Material Sciences, Systems Integration, and Test Technology Development. Amendment 1 to the NASA ARMD Research Opportunities in Aeronautics (ROA) 2008 NRA has been posted. This amendment includes new topics for Appendix B.3 Integrated Intelligent Flight Deck Technologies. We anticipate that additional amendments will be released in the future to add additional topics in other Project areas. Note: For the Integrated Intelligent Flight Deck Technologies Project, the due date for the first round of evaluations is 5/2/08. To be considered in the first round of evaluations, proposals must be received by 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time as described in Section IV(c). It is expected that the majority, and perhaps all, of the available funds will be allocated during this first round of evaluations. However, the topics in this section will remain open until 7/2/08 in case funding still remains after the first round of evaluations. A note will be posted on the ROA page on NSPIRES if funds are expended and no further reviews are expected. Proposers who decide to wait until after the first round of evaluations do so at their own risk.
