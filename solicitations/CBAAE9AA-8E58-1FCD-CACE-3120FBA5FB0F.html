Special Notices & Amendments

Notices NOTICE November 14, 2023: Proposers are advised that if they choose to use the PDF form version of the PRD, they should be sure NOT to lock the document or to &ldquo;flatten&rdquo; it in any way. Doing so may interfere with NSPIRES&rsquo;s ability to accept the proposal document and/or may cut off information that is needed. NOTICE: On September 6, 2023, at 12 pm Eastern Time, NASA will hold an informational webinar focused on the suborbital research aspects of the APRA and LCAS program elements. For connect information please refer to the Webinar informational notice, posted under other documents on the right side of this page. NOTICE: Amended August 21, 2023. This amendment releases the final text and due date for this program element, which had been listed as &quot;TBD&quot;. NOIs are not requested. The proposal due date is November 21, 2023. Proposers are strongly encouraged to use the standard Heliophysics template for Current and Pending Support for the PI and all Co-Is, regardless of time commitment and the template for the Open Science and Data Management Plan (OSDMP), see Section 2.2.2 and: https://science.nasa.gov/researchers/templates-heliophysic-division-appendix-b-roses-proposals. This program element is participating in the Inclusion Plan Pilot Program, see Section 2.2.3. This Inclusion Plan will not be part of the adjectival ratings nor selection recommendations for this opportunity. Proposers must provide a Payload Reference Document (PRD) along with the proposal to allow NASA to best match the research payload needs with multiple compatible flight platforms and identify the greatest suborbital research capabilities and launch flexibilities possible. See Section 2.2.4. The description of the specific proposal opportunity on this page is contained in the document 'B.9 Heliophysics Low Cost Access to Space'. The document B.1 Heliophysics Research Program Overview' describes research activities within the NASA science division that is managing the specific proposal opportunity on this page and may impose requirements upon proposals submitted to this program element. The document 'Summary of Solicitation' describes the common requirements for all ROSES-2023 proposal opportunities. The document 'Table 1' contains the proposal check list from the Summary of Solicitation. The documents 'Table 2' and 'Table 3' contain the list of all proposal opportunities and their due dates, sorted by (full or Step-2) proposal due date or appendix number, respectively. All of these documents are kept up to date and incorporate amendments, clarifications, and corrections in a clearly identifiable manner.
