Special Notices & Amendments

Notices NASA is completing internal approval processes prior to signature of the ACO Space Act Agreements. We anticipate industry partners having access to their Space Act Agreements via NSPIRES in early July. We ask that industry partners reframe from releasing any public news release or announcement of your selection until this process if fully complete. NASA will provide notification to partners when they are allowed to release media communication. We are looking forward to start the work under each ACO partnership. NASA is developing an updated schedule to the Standing ACO Appendix A review activities. In addition, NASA will host a webinar to provide additional information on Appendix A status. Updates to the Standing ACO review activities and a date for the webinar will be posted on NSPIRES when available. NASA STMD ACO Program would like to remind that Lead Entity proposers are required to submit PDF versions of Attachment 14 through NSPIRES and Excel Spreadsheet version to the Lead Entity Box link provided on the Appendix A Call for Proposals landing page. Coordination with NASA Centers is critical to ensure accurate submission of NASA Resources prior to the September 24th deadline. Additionally, according to Amendment 5 to Standing ACO Solicitation, the NASA Centers are no longer required to submit their Attachment 14 to the Centers Box link. Deadline extension for the Centers: Attachment 14 for the centers is now due on September 29th to the Box link. The lead entity deadline is still held to the September 24 for their Attachment 14 Amendment 1 is issued to Standing ACO Appendix A Call for Proposals 2025 NNH25ZTR002O-APPENDIX_A on September 12, 2025 Link: See Details NOTICE TO PROPOSERS: The August 29th deadline to &quot;Request Center Letter of Intent&quot; is designed to allow NASA Centers enough time to process ACO partner request through their specific Center-level process ahead of the September 24th proposal submission deadline. Interested industry partners should make every effort to make contact with a NASA Center to begin discussions on any ACO partnership proposal. There are no documents required to be submitted to NSPIRES associated with the August 29th deadline.
