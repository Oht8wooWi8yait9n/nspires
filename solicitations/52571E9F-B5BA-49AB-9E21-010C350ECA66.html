Special Notices & Amendments

Notices Amendment 14, August 2, 2006: Due to the unavailability of the NSPIRES proposal submission system on August 1-2, the due date for the ROSES program element &quot;Planetary Protection Research&quot; (Appendix C.19) has been extended until 11:59 pm Eastern time, Tuesday, August 8, 2006. Proposals submitted after the August 8 proposal due date will be handled as late proposals. NASA apologizes for any inconvenience that this situation has caused. Questions may be addressed to the NSPIRES help desk or to the program officer identified in ROSES Appendix C.19, Dr. John Rummel. Amendment 5, March 17, 2006: This program element has reduced funding for new awards in FY07.
